Sanskrit Text Explorer

Welcome to the Sanskrit Text Explorer — a tool designed for readers who are not familiar with Sanskrit or Devanagari, but want to explore classical texts with clarity, structure, and guided grammatical breakdowns.

What this tool does

  • Shows Devanagari text with transliteration
  • Breaks down compounds (sandhi) word-by-word
  • Highlights nouns, verbs, pronouns, adjectives, indeclinables
  • Shows case information for each word
  • Lets you explore verse-by-verse with grammar toggles

How to Use This Explorer (Read Me First)

Open any verse and use the Grammar Options panel on the right to peel back the verse layer by layer:

  • Separate joined words (Sandhi split): Toggle this on to break long compound words into individual words below the shloka. Compound words are underlined in the verse—hover over an underlined compound or any split word to spotlight all parts belonging together.
  • Read in IAST Transliteration: Cannot read Devanāgarī yet? Flip the slider next to Compound-split verse to switch the split text into Romanized IAST script while keeping all grammar colors and hover links active.
  • See Phrase Groups: Check Show phrase groups to wrap related words inside the compound-split verse into visual pills, showing how the verse breaks into natural thought units.
  • Word-by-Word Meanings & Grammar: Hover over any split word to see its English meaning and grammatical case. Use the color toggles on the right (Nouns, Verbs, Pronouns, Adjectives, Indeclinables, and Cases) to color-code parts of speech across every view.
  • Read in Meaning Order: Sanskrit verses arrange words for poetic meter. Toggle Read in meaning order to rearrange the phrases into natural English prose order, complete with role tags (such as Speaker, Person addressed, or What happens?).
  • Show Verbatim Translation: Toggle this on to view a direct, line-by-line English translation alongside your grammatical breakdown.
