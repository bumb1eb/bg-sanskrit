Sanskrit Text Explorer

Welcome to the Sanskrit Text Explorer — a tool designed for readers who are not familiar with Sanskrit or Devanagari, but want to explore classical texts with clarity, structure, and guided grammatical breakdowns.

What this tool does

  • Shows Devanagari text with transliteration
  • Breaks down compounds (sandhi) word-by-word
  • Highlights nouns, verbs, pronouns, adjectives, indeclinables
  • Shows case information for each word
  • Lets you explore verse-by-verse with grammar toggles

Available Texts

Śrī Bhagavad Gītā – Start from the Beginning – Resume from Last Verse
