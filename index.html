Sanskrit Text Explorer

Welcome to the Sanskrit Text Explorer — a tool designed for readers who are not familiar with Sanskrit or Devanagari, but want to explore classical texts with clarity, structure, and guided grammatical breakdowns.

What this tool does

  • Shows Devanagari text with transliteration
  • Breaks down compounds (sandhi) word-by-word
  • Highlights nouns, verbs, pronouns, adjectives, indeclinables
  • Shows case information for each word
  • Lets you explore verse-by-verse with grammar toggles

How to Use This Explorer (Read Me First)

Open any verse and use the Grammar Options panel (on the right on desktop, or below the verse on mobile) to peel back the verse layer by layer:

  • Separate joined words (Split Words): Toggle this on to open the interactive breakdown panel right below the shloka. Compound words are underlined in the verse—below, their split parts appear with a dotted underline and + joiners. Hover over any underlined compound or split word to spotlight all parts belonging together.
  • Switch between Devanāgarī and IAST Transliteration: Cannot read Devanāgarī yet? Flip the Devanāgarī / Transliteration slider at the top of the breakdown panel to read in Romanized IAST script across every view.
  • Step through Phrase Groups & Meaning Order: Use the 3-step mode bar at the top of the panel (Split Words → Phrase Groups → Meaning Order) or the matching checkboxes in Grammar Options:
    • Phrase Groups wraps related words into numbered parchment pills (1, 2, 3…) in original verse order.
    • Meaning Order rearranges those numbered pills into natural English prose order with role tags and phrase meanings.
  • Word-by-Word Meanings & Grammar: Hover over (or tap) any split word to see its English meaning. Use the color toggles in Grammar Options (Nouns, Verbs, Pronouns, Adjectives, Indeclinables, and Cases) to color-code parts of speech across every view.
  • Show Verbatim Translation: Toggle this on to view a direct, line-by-line English translation below your grammatical breakdown.
